Renewal

Stripe charges the card you used at checkout again on the renewal date. Weekly, monthly, and 3-month plans renew on their own schedule. You do not need to pay again by hand while the plan stays active.

Cancellation

You can cancel from Cafy billing settings, or from the Stripe billing page linked there. If the plan is active, cancellation waits until {{renewal_date}}. You keep access until then, and that period is not charged again. Cancelling does not refund the period you already paid.

If a charge fails

A failed renewal is marked past due. Paid features pause until the charge succeeds. Stripe retries that invoice automatically. Update your card in billing settings so a later attempt can go through. If you cancel while a payment is past due, Cafy cancels straight away and voids the open invoice, so Stripe stops retrying it.

A failed upgrade from Jobs Premium to full Premium is rolled back to Jobs Premium. The failed upgrade invoice is voided and is not retried at the full price.
